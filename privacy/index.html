Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Wyckademy publishes free education about the Wyckoff method of reading stock charts, through our website, email and social media channels. This page explains what personal information we handle and why.

What we collect

We only collect the email address (and first name, if given) of people who choose to sign up for our emails. We do not ask for any other personal information.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send the emails you signed up for, such as the daily trade email. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. Emails are sent through our email provider, Resend, which stores the addresses on our behalf.

Our use of YouTube and other platforms

Our publishing tools use the YouTube Data API, the Instagram API and the TikTok Content Posting API only to upload and manage videos on our own Wyckademy channels. They do not access, collect or store data about viewers or other users. Our use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access our tools have been given to a Google account can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep email addresses only while you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details, we remove them within 30 days. Our publishing tools store no YouTube, Instagram or TikTok user data, so there is nothing of yours to delete there.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: trade@wyckademy.com.

Wyckademy content is for education only and is not investment advice.
