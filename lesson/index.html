The spring

The last shake-out below support before smart money lets price rise. Phase C of accumulation.

▶ YouTube · lesson video

What a spring is

After a selling climax, price settles into a trading range while large buyers quietly absorb the remaining supply. A spring is a brief drop below the bottom of that range. It triggers stop-losses and tempts late short sellers, and then price closes back inside the range.

If the drop finds little new supply, the selling is exhausted. That is the moment the Composite Man has been waiting for: the path of least resistance turns up.

How Tom confirms one

  1. A clear mark-down came first, ending in a selling climax on heavy volume.
  2. An automatic rally and secondary tests built a range that lasted weeks.
  3. Price broke support, then closed back inside the range on the same day.
  4. Volume on the break stayed modest: no wave of new supply.
